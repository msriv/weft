We don't decide what's true.

Weft shows who reported what, when, and from where. We flag places where reports don't line up. Every flag comes from a written rule you can read below.

What we collect

News outlets
From every country involved, in their own language. State-run outlets are labelled as state-run everywhere they appear.
Official documents
Government releases, parliament and court records. Each is labelled by who issued it, and whether that body is a party to the story.
Blogs and analysts
Included, weighted lower, and always marked as opinion or analysis.

We store summaries and links, not full articles. Every node links to its original.

How a source's weight is set

Weight only changes the order things appear in. No report is ever hidden. We never score a source on whether it was “right”, only on how it reports.

The anomaly rules

Anomalies compare reports with other reports, never with “the truth”. No AI model flags anomalies. For the first hours of a story they stay provisional, until at least 5 sources from 2 perspectives have reported.

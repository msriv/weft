Name options

WEFTPICK
In weaving, the warp is the fixed threads and the weft is woven across them. Here the public record is the warp and every narrative is weft. It's the UI, as a word. Short and easy to say.
Parallax
The same object looks different from different positions. The clearest pitch for "every side's press", but heavily used by other companies.
Sightline
An unobstructed line of view. Calm and trustworthy, but less distinctive.
Undercurrent
What moves beneath the headlines. Moody and fits the dark look, but long and a little ominous for a trust product.
